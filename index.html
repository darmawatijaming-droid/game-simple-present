Simple Present Tense
Used to express habits, general facts, and universal truths that happen regularly or are always true.
Affirmative (+)
S + V(s/es) + O

Negative (−)
S + do/does + not + V1 + O

Interrogative (?)
Do/Does + S + V1 + O + ?
Does she read a book daily ?
do → I / You / We / They  |  does → He / She / It
